How to Play

  • Aim and launch. Drag from the launch pad in the direction you want to fly — further out is more power — then let go. Arrow keys and Space work too.
  • Gravity does the steering. Every planet, star and void pulls on the probe. The dotted line previews only the first couple of seconds, so the rest of the arc is yours to predict.
  • One burn per attempt. While flying, press Space or tap the field once to fire a boost along your current heading. It is often the difference between a near miss and a docking.
  • Nothing is solid but the station. Touching a planet, a star, a void or a barrier ends the attempt, and so does drifting off the map.
  • Collect the crystals on the way in. They only count if you reach the station in the same attempt — and fewer attempts always scores higher.
