How to Play

  • Fly with the arrow keys or WASD. On a touchscreen, drag anywhere on the field — the ship follows your finger and fires by itself.
  • Fire with Space. Big rocks take three hits and break into smaller ones, and the small ones are worth the most.
  • Chain kills to build your combo multiplier. Taking a hit resets it, so a careful run outscores a reckless one.
  • Grab power-ups dropped by broken rocks: Shield absorbs one impact, Rapid triples your fire rate, Pulse clears the screen.
  • Three hulls. Waves get faster every twenty seconds and never stop coming — the run ends when the last hull goes.
